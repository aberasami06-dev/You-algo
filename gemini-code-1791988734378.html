What Is the YouTube Algorithm?

The YouTube algorithm is a collection of recommendation systems that decide which videos to show to viewers, where to show them, and when to show them.

Tip #1

Recommendation Signals

YouTube evaluates viewer history, search queries, clicked videos, watch duration, and feedback (likes, dislikes).

Tip #2

Multiple Systems

Separate discovery models exist for Home Feed, Search, Suggested Videos, and Shorts Feed.

Tip #3

Click-Through Rate (CTR)

The percentage of people who click after seeing your thumbnail + title packaging.

Tip #4

Audience Retention

First 30 seconds dictate whether YouTube continues expanding impression tests to broader audiences.

💡 Sami Abera's Golden Creator Rule

Don't ask: “How can I make YouTube's algorithm promote my video?”

Ask: “How can I make a video that the right audience wants to click, watch, enjoy, and watch more of?”

What Is the YouTube Algorithm?

The YouTube algorithm is a real-time recommendation engine designed to match individual viewers with videos they are most likely to watch and enjoy.

Signal 01

Recommendation Signals

YouTube evaluates watch history, search queries, clicked thumbnails, watch duration, and satisfaction feedback (likes, shares).

Signal 02

Multi-System Engine

Separate recommendation models govern the Home Feed, Suggested Videos sidebar, Search Results, and YouTube Shorts Feed.

Signal 03

Click-Through Rate (CTR)

The proportion of users who click your video thumbnail after seeing it. Strong packaging (Title + Thumbnail) drives high initial CTR.

Signal 04

Retention & APV

Average Percentage Viewed (APV) and retention hooks in the first 30 seconds decide whether YouTube expands impression tests.

💡 Sami Abera's Golden Creator Rule

Don't ask: “How can I trick YouTube's algorithm into promoting my video?”
Ask: “How can I make a video that my target audience wants to click, watch, enjoy, and share?”
